Transformers (2007)

> "Freedom is the right of all sentient beings."

## Overview
**Transformers** is a 2007 American science fiction action film directed by Michael Bay, based on the toy line by Hasbro.

### Main Characters
* **Sam Witwicky** - Played by Shia LaBeouf
* **Optimus Prime** - Voiced by Peter Cullen

### Key Elements
1. The AllSpark
2. Autobots vs. Decepticons
3. The battle for Earth
